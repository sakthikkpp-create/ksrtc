🗣️ Speak Like a Local

Essential Malayalam phrases with instant audio pronunciation

"Namaskaram" (നമസ്കാരം) Hello / Greetings of respect
"Evideyanu bus?" (എവിടെയാണ് ബസ്?) Where is the bus parked / arriving?
"Ethra roopa aanu?" (എത്ര രൂപയാണ്?) How much is the fare?
"Nanni!" (നന്ദി!) Thank you very much!
"Vazhi paranjutharumo?" (വഴി പറഞ്ഞുതരുമോ?) Could you please guide my way?
